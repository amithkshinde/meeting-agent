Updates now install themselves: press Download once and the app replaces itself and reopens.

- No more downloading a disk image, dragging the app and choosing Replace. Your calls and settings stay.
- It never restarts in the middle of a recording; it waits until the call ends.
- If this Mac doesn't allow the app to replace itself, the installer opens as before.
- Includes the Screen Recording and password-window fixes from 0.3.1 and 0.3.2.

Released 23 November 2026.
