Questions

Before you download.

The short answers. Anything else, write to me from the Contact section.

Is it really free?

Yes. Meeting Agent itself is free and open source under the MIT License. For chat, recaps and summaries you bring your own AI key from a provider such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Any usage cost is between you and that provider.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Hindi, including calls where people switch between them.

Which call apps does it work with?

It detects calls on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and similar apps running on your computer, including calls that were never on your calendar.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. It lives in meeting-agent-windows. There is a ready-made installer (.exe). Windows may warn that the app is unsigned: click More info, then Run anyway.

Should I tell people I am recording?

Yes. Recording rules differ by country and company, so tell participants and get their consent before you record.

Can I send feedback?

There is a Feedback section on this page and inside the app. You can stay anonymous, or leave your name and email if you would like a reply.
